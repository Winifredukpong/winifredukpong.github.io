About

Purchases are emotional first, logical second.

People rarely buy the product. They buy what it lets them feel, avoid, or become.

I’m a research-driven copy strategist. Before I write a headline, I read your reviews, study your customers’ own words and look at where your page loses them. Then I write copy that speaks to what they actually want.

I work with growing brands that already have customers and traffic, and need sharper positioning and more persuasive messaging to turn visitors into buyers.

How I think

What I look for before I write a word.

Customer language

The exact words buyers use in reviews, comments and support chats often outperform anything invented in a brainstorm.

The real objection

Most hesitation isn’t about price. It’s about trust, risk or doubt that the product works. I find which one it is.

The friction points

Vague headlines, buried benefits and weak calls to action quietly cost sales. I mark each one and fix it.
